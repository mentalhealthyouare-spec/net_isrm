# ISRM Insight Hub & Customisable Network Services — Client Description

**Document Status:** Client-Safe Scope & Architecture Description (`v1.3`)  
**Target Entity:** Institute of Strategic Risk Management (ISRM) — Ukraine Chapter & Global Leadership  
**Baseline Dataset:** 178-record ISRM Ukraine Community Register & Knowledge Map (`2026-08-28` snapshot)

---

## 1. Executive Purpose & Innovation Proposition

The **ISRM Insight Hub** is a secure, multi-community web platform and interactive service-graph configurator. It transforms static, fragmented chapter contact lists into a governed, searchable expert ecosystem with verifiable credentials, wartime resilience case studies, and structured institutional partnerships.

**Core Innovation Proposition:**  
Rather than relying on ad-hoc personal memory or unverified social media follower counts, the tool makes **expert discovery, operational knowledge capture, evidence review, and network mobilisation repeatable with significantly fewer manual resources**. The bounded pilot is designed to measure this operational efficiency empirically rather than promising speculative financial or membership forecasts.

---

## 2. Single-Screen Service Graph & Four Sequential Packages

The platform operates on a single continuous graph workspace across three synchronized interaction modes (**NETWORK / PROTOTYPE**, **PLATFORM CONFIGURATOR**, and **DEPENDENCIES**) structured around four sequential costing rings:

1. **Package 1 (Ring 1) — Discovery, CRM Audit & Governance Consulting:**  
   Establishes the 31-field controlled CRM data dictionary, separates raw source records from verified identities, aligns the taxonomy with aggregate LinkedIn Page analytics (8,742 followers), and defines the data-controller and operational security rules.
2. **Package 2 (Ring 2) — Application MVP & Structural Foundation:**  
   Delivers the non-removable platform core: the interactive network graph, the four upper filter drawers (`FILTERS`, `SECTORS`, `TERRITORIES`, `EXPERT NETWORK`), the isolated Cluster Layer for 133 unverified/partial records, and the accessible `Connections List`.
3. **Package 3 (Ring 3) — Bounded Pilot Functions & Repeatable Expert Engagement Cycle:**  
   Contains the **closed-loop, repeatable expert engagement cycle** across two cohorts:
   - **Cohort 1:** Existing engaged ISRM Ukraine members (45 verified profiles researched from public sources).
   - **Cohort 2:** A second expert cohort to be defined and approved by the Client/Board prior to survey or pilot launch.
   - *Note:* Only Ring 3 contains this repeatable cycle (`Identify → Consensual Survey/Verify → Case Interview & Security Review → Knowledge/Referral Output`). Repeating the cycle on subsequent cohorts or chapters does not require a fifth software package.
4. **Package 4 (Ring 4) — Trust & Influence Index (TII), Verified Projects & Global Continuation:**  
   Extends the validated platform with the proposed **Trust & Influence Index (TII)**, the Verified Projects registry, official LinkedIn Page aggregate XLS crosswalks, and multi-chapter federation across ISRM’s 35 global chapters.

---

## 3. Trust & Influence Index (TII) Methodology Boundary

The **Trust & Influence Index (TII)** is a proposed evidence-structuring framework—not an automated black-box ranking or unverified popularity score.
- **Trust Pillar:** Recorded strictly on the basis of defined checks (source URL, verification date, evidence type, confidence level, human reviewer sign-off, and an explicit correction/appeal path).
- **Influence Pillar:** Evaluates relevant publications, keynote contributions, and confirmed institutional reach normalized by sector and period—protecting against substituting media visibility for substantive risk expertise.
- **Projects Pillar:** Links experts to confirmed crisis, humanitarian, or reconstruction projects, recording role, timeframe, and verified outcome.

Until the Board formally approves the weighting and normalization rules, TII remains marked `UNDER DEVELOPMENT` with zero automated ranking or access restrictions.

## 4. Two-Stage Document & Commercial Governance

To maintain strict commercial clarity, the configuration process separates technical scope validation from legal contracting:
- **Stage 1 Document (Output of this Configurator):** *Validated Scope & Indicative Budget Range* — records selected structural components, optional features, exclusions, dependencies, delivery stages, and indicative delivery corridors for confirmation.
- **Stage 2 Document (Post-Client Confirmation):** *Commercial Proposal & Statement of Work (SOW)* — specifies final fixed pricing, named delivery team, acceptance criteria, IP provisions, and binding payment schedule.

**Indicative Pricing & Payment Terms:**  
All client-facing package prices are indicative pending preliminary client approval of scope and an acceptable overall budget ceiling. Payment is due upon delivery and formal acceptance of each sequential package, with an initial mobilisation payment of **at least 30%** of the total agreed budget.
