## 1. Accepted Route & Journey Separation

The application enforces strict separation between the **Partner Journey** and the **Client Journey**:

### Client Journey (`/?path=client`)
- **Step 1: Client Description** — Presents the executive narrative, 178-record Ukraine baseline, 4 sequential packages, bounded pilot governance, and Trust & Influence Index (TII) methodology. Contains zero partner fee splits or links to partner routes.
- **Step 2: Client Calculations** — Displays client-safe aggregate package corridors (Lean vs. Expanded delivery models), relationship model selectors (Models A, B, C), non-linear minimum team capacity rules, and indicative 30%+ mobilisation payment terms.
- **Step 3: Client Application Workspace** — Single-screen interactive service-graph workspace with `NETWORK`, `CONFIGURATOR`, and `DEPENDENCIES` modes, plus the non-submitting editable Survey Preview and Print/PDF Validated Scope export.

### Partner Journey (`/?path=partner`)
- **Step 1: Partner Description** — Details partner-to-partner and partner-to-client governance (ISRM, Frishberg & Partners, Technical/Architecture Lead, Analytical/OSINT Lead), IP/reuse boundaries, and pilot safety gates. Includes read-only inspection of the Client-Safe Description without crossing into the client route.
- **Step 2: Partner Calculations** — Contains editable Partner Role Fee Inputs across Rings 1–4 alongside the live Client-Safe Aggregate Calculation view. Any edit to a partner fee immediately recalculates the corresponding client package line and total.
- **Step 3: Partner Workspace** — Full single-screen graph workspace with internal CRM engagement pipeline visibility (`F&P opportunity`, `ISRM opportunity`, `Next action`) and Loop Quality Gate audit checklist.

---

## 2. Four Sequential Costing Rings & Pilot Cycle Boundary

1. **Ring 1 (Package 1):** Consulting, Discovery, 178-Record CRM Audit & Governance
2. **Ring 2 (Package 2):** Application MVP & Structural Foundation (Non-removable base)
3. **Ring 3 (Package 3):** Bounded Pilot Functions — **exclusively contains the Repeatable Expert Engagement Cycle** (`Cohort Selection → Consensual Survey/Verify → Case Interview & Security Review → Knowledge & Referral Output`). Repetition on Cohort 2 or other chapters does not create a 5th costing package.
4. **Ring 4 (Package 4):** Trust & Influence Index (TII), Verified Projects Ledger & 35-Chapter Global Expansion

---

## 3. Commercial & Governance Gates

- **Indicative Pricing Gate:** All client-facing figures are marked indicative pending preliminary approval of scope and an acceptable overall budget ceiling.
- **Delivery Payment Terms:** Payment is due upon delivery/acceptance of each sequential package; initial mobilisation payment is at least 30% of the total agreed price.
- **Cohort 2 & Survey Gate:** Cohort 1 comprises existing engaged ISRM Ukraine members (45 verified profiles). Cohort 2 is held as a formal Client/Board decision gate. The embedded Application-Need Survey is an editable, non-submitting preview until Board approval of controller, privacy notice, and retention rules.
